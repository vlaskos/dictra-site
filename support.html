How do I add the Dictra keyboard?

Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, and choose Dictra. Then switch to it from the globe icon in any app.

Why does it need Full Access?

Full Access lets the keyboard reach the microphone so it can hear your dictation. It is used only for that. Dictra does not log keystrokes or send anything over the network.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Dictra downloads one speech model the first time you use it. After that, dictation and translation run on your iPhone with no internet connection.

Which languages are supported?

Dictation in Ukrainian, Russian, Polish, and English. Translation works in any direction between them — speak in one language and insert in another.

Where is my dictation history stored?

Only on your device. You can clear it any time in the app, and deleting the app removes it. Nothing is uploaded.

The keyboard can't hear me

Check that Full Access is on for Dictra in Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Dictra, and that Microphone access is allowed for the app.
