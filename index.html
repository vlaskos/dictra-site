The idea

A keyboard that types what you say

Dictra turns your voice into text in any app, and can translate it on the way in. It sets up in five short steps, and after that dictation and translation run entirely on your iPhone.

  1. Welcome — voice to text, on your phone.
  2. Allow the microphone.
  3. Add the keyboard, turn on Full Access.
  4. Download a speech model, then work offline.
  5. Tap the mic and talk.

Private by design

It stays on your iPhone

Dictate into any app and every word is turned to text on the device. Your history is saved only on your iPhone — no account, no cloud, nothing uploaded. Wipe it in a tap whenever you want.

Four languages, any direction

Speak Ukrainian, insert English

Talk in one language and the keyboard inserts another — any direction between Ukrainian, Russian, Polish, and English. The translation runs on the device too, so nothing you say leaves the phone.

  • Ukrainian → English
  • Polish → Ukrainian
  • Russian → Polish
  • English → Ukrainian

Looks right everywhere

Light or dark. Your call.

Dictra follows your system appearance and ships both light and dark themes, tuned so the accents stay easy to read either way. The app itself is localized in English and Ukrainian.

How it works

From voice to text in three taps

Dictra is a keyboard extension, so it works inside any app: messages, notes, email, search. Switch to it like any other keyboard, then talk.

  1. Add the Dictra keyboard in Settings and allow Full Access so it can hear you.
  2. Open any app, switch to Dictra, and tap the mic. Speak the way you talk.
  3. Your words appear as text. Turn on Translate to insert another language instead.

One speech model downloads on first launch. After that, dictation and translation work offline.
