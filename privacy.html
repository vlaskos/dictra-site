Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Dictra does not collect your personal data. Here is exactly what that means.

No data collection

Dictra has no account, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. We never receive your voice, your transcripts, or your translations.

On-device processing

When you dictate, audio from the microphone is processed on your iPhone to produce text, then discarded. Speech recognition and translation run locally on the device. Your audio and text are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

Your dictation history

Dictra can keep a history of your past dictations so you can reuse them. It is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage shared with the Dictra keyboard. You can clear it at any time in the app, and deleting the app removes it.

Microphone and Full Access

The keyboard needs "Allow Full Access" so it can reach the microphone to hear your dictation. Full Access is used only for that. The keyboard does not log your keystrokes or send anything over the network.

Speech models

The first time you use Dictra, it downloads a speech recognition model from its provider (Hugging Face, for the WhisperKit and Parakeet models) so recognition can run offline afterward. This download retrieves the model only; it does not send your audio, text, or identity.

Translation

Translation uses Apple's on-device translation, which runs on your iPhone. Apple may download language packs to your device; see Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles that. Dictra does not send your text to its own servers.

No data sharing

Because Dictra collects no personal data, there is nothing to sell, share, or disclose.

Children

Dictra is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
